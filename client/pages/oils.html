Oils guide

Find the right oil for your porosity

Oils don't moisturize on their own — they seal moisture in. Pick one that matches how easily your hair absorbs and holds water.

Low porosity

Lightweight oils

Jojoba, grapeseed, and light argan oil sit close to the hair's natural sebum and won't add buildup. Apply to soaking-wet hair.

Medium porosity

Balanced oils

Sweet almond, apricot kernel, and sunflower oil absorb well without over-sealing. A few drops on damp hair is usually enough.

High porosity

Heavier, sealing oils

Castor, avocado, and shea-heavy blends slow down moisture loss from raised cuticles. Use after a leave-in conditioner.

Scalp

Scalp-only oils

Rosemary and tea tree (diluted in a carrier oil) are popular for scalp massage — patch test first, and skip if you have broken skin.
