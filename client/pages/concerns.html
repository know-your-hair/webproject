Hair concerns

Solutions for specific concerns

General starting points — persistent or worsening issues deserve a dermatologist's opinion.

Concern

Breakage

Usually protein-moisture imbalance or heat damage. Cut back on heat styling, alternate protein and moisture treatments, and trim damaged ends.

Concern

Dryness

Check your porosity first — the right products differ by porosity type. Add a leave-in and seal with an appropriate oil.

Concern

Thinning / shedding

Some daily shedding is normal (50–100 hairs). Sudden increases can relate to stress, diet, or hormones — worth a dermatologist visit if it persists.

Concern

Premature Graying

Early gray hair can be caused by genetics, age, or other factors. If graying happens suddenly or increases quickly, consult a dermatologist.

Concern

Frizz

Often a sign hair wants more moisture or a smoother cuticle. Try a leave-in plus a light gel or cream, and avoid brushing dry hair.

Concern

Product buildup

Clarify every 2–4 weeks if you use silicones, dry shampoo, or styling gels regularly.

Concern

Color-treated damage

Bleached or heavily colored hair behaves like high-porosity hair — prioritize protein-moisture balance and minimize additional heat.
