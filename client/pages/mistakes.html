Common mistakes

Pitfalls that undo a good routine

Most hair damage comes from a handful of repeated habits, not one bad product.

01

Washing too often (or not enough). Over-washing strips natural oils; under-washing lets product and sebum build up. Match frequency to your scalp, not a schedule.

02

Skipping heat protectant. Any tool over 300°F/150°C needs protectant first — even "quick touch-ups."

03

Brushing wet hair with the wrong brush. Hair is weakest when wet. Use a wide-tooth comb, starting from the ends.

04

Tight hairstyles, all the time. Constant tension causes traction alopecia at the hairline over time. Rotate styles and loosen edges.

05

Sleeping on cotton. Cotton pillowcases absorb moisture and create friction. Satin or silk reduces both.

06

Ignoring protein-moisture balance. Too much protein makes hair stiff; too little makes it mushy and weak. If hair feels "off," check which one you've been overdoing.

07

Skipping regular trims. Regular trims help remove split ends and keep damaged hair from getting worse.
