Hair Porosity Test

Porosity is how well your hair absorbs and holds onto moisture. Run either test below with a clean strand of hair, then tell us what you saw.

Float test

Does it sink or float?

  1. Take a clean strand of hair from your brush or comb.
  2. Fill a glass with room-temperature water.
  3. Place the strand gently on the surface.
  4. Watch it for 2–4 minutes.

Spray test

How fast does it absorb water?

  1. Take a small section of clean, dry hair.
  2. Spray a fine mist of water onto it.
  3. Watch how the hair reacts.
