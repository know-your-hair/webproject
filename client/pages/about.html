About

Why KnowYourHair exists

Most hair advice online is generic. We built a small, free set of tools to help you understand your own hair's porosity and scalp needs, then act on it — nothing more, nothing less.

What you can do here

Take a porosity test, build your hair routine, and learn about oils, ingredients, common mistakes, and hair concerns.

What this site isn't

A replacement for a dermatologist. If something about your scalp or hair loss concerns you, please see a professional — we say this throughout the site, not just here.
