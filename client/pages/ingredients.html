Ingredients

What's actually in your products

Labels are easier to read once you know what a handful of common ingredients actually do.

Look for

Glycerin

A humectant that draws moisture into the hair — great in humid climates, can dry hair out in very low humidity.

Look for

Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5)

Coats the strand, improves elasticity, and reduces breakage without heavy buildup.

Use sparingly

Protein (hydrolyzed keratin, wheat protein)

Strengthens weak, damaged hair — but too much makes hair stiff and brittle. Alternate with moisture-focused products.

Watch for

Sulfates (SLS/SLES)

Strong cleansers that can strip natural oils. Fine occasionally, but a sulfate-free shampoo is gentler for dry or high-porosity hair.

Watch for

Silicones (non-water-soluble)

Give instant shine and smoothness but can build up over time without a clarifying wash.

Look for

Fatty alcohols (cetyl, stearyl)

Not the same as drying alcohols — these condition and soften. Common in good conditioners.
